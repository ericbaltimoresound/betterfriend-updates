# BetterFriend 0.7.0

- Adds **My people**, an optional shortcut list for up to ten important people.
- Makes My people easy to reach from the menu-bar window and offers it once
  after the first successful manual scan. It requires no Track or cadence setup.
- Lets you choose from ten editable draft styles before opening Messages.
- Keeps My people separate from Today recommendations, cadence, and reminders.
- Improves first-run guidance for Messages sync, Full Disk Access, standard
  macOS accounts, relaunching, and scan failures.
- Carries My people between Macs only when you explicitly include the people
  category in an encrypted profile. Matching uses anonymous tokens; names,
  phone numbers, and email addresses are not exported.
- Strengthens import previews, recovery copies, accessibility, narrow-screen
  layouts, and dark-mode contrast.

BetterFriend still never sends messages for you. Message content remains on
your Mac and is never attached to update checks or feedback.
